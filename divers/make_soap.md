# The Iodine Value

Characteristic value for soap making. The more unsaturated fatty acids an oil contains, the higher its iodine value.  
Based on this value, oils are classified as light, medium, and heavy oils.  
With an iodine value below one hundred, they are referred to as heavy, fatty oils.  
These make the soap harder. The higher the iodine value, the "drier" an oil is.  
At the same time, the iodine value allows conclusions about the shelf life of the oil.  
The higher it is, the more sensitive the oil reacts with oxygen and the shorter its shelf life.

The following classification is often mentioned:

* Fatty oils: Iodine value < 100  
* Medium oils: Iodine value 100 to 120  
* Light oils: Iodine value above 120  

# Water Amount

Rule of thumb: Water amount approx. 30–35% of the total fat amount.  
* Ratio: Usually 2:1 or 2.5:1 (i.e. 2–2.5 parts water to 1 part NaOH).  
* Example: For 1000 g oil/fat, about 300–350 ml water is needed.  
* Note: Less water makes the soap harden faster, but too little water can cause stirring problems.

# Possible Additives and Their Effects

| Additive | Amount (per 1000 g oil) | Effect |
|----------|------------------------|--------|
| Beeswax | 1–5 % | Increases hardness and longevity |
| Honey | 1–2 tbsp | Moisturizing, slightly coloring |
| Coffee | 1–2 tbsp | Exfoliating effect, slightly brown coloring |
| Oat flakes | 1–2 tbsp | Exfoliating effect, gentle on the skin |
| Milk powder | 1–2 tbsp | Nourishing, increases lather |
| Activated charcoal | 1 tsp | Coloring (black), detoxifying |
| Silk (Tussah silk) | 1 tsp dissolved in lye | Silky lather, smooth texture |
| Herbs | As desired | Aesthetics, slight exfoliating effect |
| Clay (e.g. kaolin) | 1 tsp | Gently cleanses, improves texture |
| Resin (e.g. spruce resin) | 0.5–1 tsp dissolved in oil | Light scent, skin caring, antiseptic |

* Note: Resin must be dissolved in oil beforehand, otherwise it may form lumps. Always stir additives in after reaching “trace”.  
* Handmade soap is usually superfatted by 3 to 8 percent.

# Safety Measures

* Wear safety goggles and gloves.  
* Always slowly stir sodium hydroxide into the water (never the other way around!).  
* Work in a well-ventilated room and avoid inhaling fumes if possible.

# Recipe

* Add caustic soda to the water and dissolve it in a clean metal container, caution, it gets hot!  
* Warm oil, resin, wax ... until liquid and strain if necessary.  
* When everything is approx. 40–50 degrees or cooler, mix the lye into the oil while stirring well. Ideally use a stick blender.  
* Add fragrance oil as late as possible.  
* Pour into silicone molds, let rest 24h.  
* Remove from mold and store for 4 weeks.  
* Voilà
